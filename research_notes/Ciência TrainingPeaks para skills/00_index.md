# TrainingPeaks Coach Blog — "Science" category: full inventory (index)

Scope: https://www.trainingpeaks.com/coach-blog/category/science/ only. Compiled 2026-09-30 by scraping (1) the category listing page, (2) the site's own "load more" endpoint (`admin-ajax.php`, action `tp_loadmore_ajax_handler`, tag_id 117 in category `coaching`, max_page 24) and (3) the WordPress RSS feed for the `science` tag (`/feed/?tag=science&paged=N`, 25 pages), then fetching each of the 233 article pages for the byline (`About <author>` box), `article:published_time` and the meta description. One-sentence summaries below are the article's own meta description / feed excerpt (lightly trimmed), not a reading of the body.

Method notes / caveats:
- "Science" is technically a WordPress *tag* (id 117) rendered as a category page filtered to the `coaching` category; `/page/2/` style URLs return an empty listing (infinite scroll is used), so the URL-pagination suggested in the brief does not work.
- Three sources were unioned because none is complete alone: RSS had 228 coach-blog items but omitted 5 of the newest posts; the ajax endpoint returned 219 (excludes the 16 cards rendered server-side on page 1); page 1 had 16 cards. Union = 233 unique URLs.
- The RSS `dc:creator` field is the WP uploader (e.g. "Josh Lawton" on 220 items), NOT the byline; authors below come from the article page's author box (meta `author` on newer posts is also the editor, "Molly Sughroue").
- Several older posts carry a `published_time` that reflects a later republish/SEO refresh (e.g. "All About the Mean Max Power Curve" shows 2025-11 while its image asset dates from 2015); treat dates as "date on page".
- One entry ("Virtual Coaching for Virtual Racing", 2020) now redirects to the product page https://www.trainingpeaks.com/virtual/ and has no author; counted but flagged.

### Inferences
- For a triathlon coaching skill set, the category is strongest as a source for HRV/recovery, strength-for-endurance, injury management, fuelling basics and athlete psychology; it is weak as a source for threshold testing protocols, intensity distribution and durability/pacing models, which will need to come from other TrainingPeaks categories or external literature.
- Given the 2019–2022 concentration, nutrition guidance (e.g. the 90 g/h article from 2022) and heat guidance likely predate more recent consensus shifts and should be date-flagged in any KB.

### Gaps
- Theme tags are single-label and assigned from title/excerpt; some articles straddle themes (e.g. hydration & bone-stress could be strength & injury; disordered-eating pieces could be medical). Counts would shift by a few items under a different rubric.
- Whether the sibling categories (e.g. "physiology", "training", "nutrition" under coach-blog) contain the missing threshold/periodization material was outside scope and not checked.
