# IRONMAN 70.3 Vitoria-Gasteiz (11 July 2027)

Official GPX files from https://www.ironman.com/races/im703-vitoria/course (downloaded 2026-10-07): `swim.gpx` (1.9 km,
Ulibarri-Gamboa / Landa lake), `bike.gpx` (one 90.0 km loop, Landa to Vitoria, 575 m of ascent on a plateau at 509-616 m),
`run.gpx` (two loops downtown, 21.2 km). The files also hold waypoints (aid stations) that the readers skip.
`build_course.py` writes `course_summary.json` and the weather window (ERA5, 1-21 July, 07:00-17:00, 2016-2025, at Vitoria,
Landa and the bike high point). Hot: the hourly 90th percentile in the race window is 28 °C (organiser: highs up to 33 °C).
